About

Francesca Iannucci

Hello! I'm Francesca, an emerging trans filmmaker based in London, with a multifaceted background in directing, cinematography and editing.

I'm known for a cursed style of dark comedy. I directed the sci-fi short Break the Cycle (2024) and the very cursed It’s Really Beautiful Out (2021), contributed to high-profile British films such as One Life (2023), and I'm currently finishing my feature debut, The Ice Lolly King (2026).
